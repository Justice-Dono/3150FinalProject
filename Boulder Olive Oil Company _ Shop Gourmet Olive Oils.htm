Crafting & curating unique gourmet products in Boulder since 2022

Boulder Olive Oil Company is Colorado’s go-to source for the finest extra virgin olive oils and balsamic vinegars, along with fine cheeses, spices, culinary gifts, wholesale options and much more at our flagship store in Boulder.

We believe real, fresh ingredients speak for themselves. We craft our infused balsamic vinegars using balsamic vinegar imported from Modena, Italy fused with fresh fruit and produce, and nothing else added. More difficult? Definitely! But we guarantee you can taste the difference between real fruit and the flavorings or extracts used by others.

To source our incredible olive oils, each year our team visits farms in Greece, Italy, Spain and across the US to taste dozens of olive oils, from Koroneiki to Arbequina. We also partner with small companies like our local Boulder friends at Wildly Virgin to showcase their Gold medal award-winning small batch Portuguese varietals.

We now offer wholesale extra virgin olive oils, fruit-infused balsamic vinegars, and handcrafted salt and spice blends, in perfect sizes for your retail store including our bestselling TSA-friendly bottles.

We hope to meet you in person in Boulder at our shop, at your local farmers market, or during the 2027 Sundance Film Festival - please reach out anytime to say hello!
